OUR COMMUNITY CODE

Rules of Alfheim

A shared world is only as strong as the community that builds it.

THE SPIRIT OF ALFHEIM
Adventure boldly, build proudly, and respect the people sharing the realm with you.
01

Respect Every Viking

Treat players respectfully. Harassment, threats, discrimination, and abusive behavior are not welcome.

02

Protect Builds & Belongings

Do not steal, destroy, modify, or enter other players’ builds or storage without permission.

03

Play Fair

Do not exploit bugs, cheat, grief, or deliberately disrupt the experience of others.

04

Build Responsibly

Be considerate when building near others. Avoid blocking important shared routes or community areas.

05

Keep the Peace

Settle disputes respectfully. If a situation cannot be resolved, contact an administrator.

06

Follow Admin Direction

Respect reasonable instructions from server administrators. Rules may be updated as the community grows.

Questions or Problems?

Contact a current administrator if you need clarification or want to report a concern. Serious or repeated violations may result in removal from the server.
