Getting Started

01 · Get Valheim

Purchase and install Valheim through Steam if you do not already own it.

STEAM STORE

02 · Get the Password

Alfheim is password-protected. Reach out to a current player or an administrator for access.

03 · Review the Rules

Read the community rules before joining and help keep Alfheim welcoming.

VIEW RULES

04 · Join Alfheim

From the Home page, choose Enter Alfheim and confirm the Steam launch. Your browser may request permission to open Steam. Enter the server password when Valheim asks.
